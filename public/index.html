The Cut of Him

Six-foot-seven, three-twenty-five. 55 years old. Mottled, leathered skin. Piercing black eyes. Dark hair gone grey at the temples. Beard kept short — long ones catch on rope.

Wears canvas trousers, leather vest over a stained linen shirt, no armor he can't swim in. The flask on his hip, the wooden narwhal in his pocket.

"Reluctant hero, quietly self-destructive, deeply competent on the water."

Ideals · Bonds · Flaws

Personality: Brooding, reclusive, stern.

Responsibility: "When lives are on the line, I act. Excuses don't keep people from drowning."

Protection: "The strong are meant to shield those who can't shield themselves."

Bond: Douglas Barker's compass. It always seems to know when bad weather is coming.

Flaw: Trusts signs, tools, and instincts more easily than he trusts people.

How He Lives Now
  • Roof. A small shack near the docks.
  • Boat. A single rowboat. The keelboat went years ago, after Barker.
  • Day work. Ferries fishermen for the Solmor fleet when Anders has need.
  • Night work. Bouncer at The Snapping Line. Trouble usually ends quickly.
  • The carving. He whittles small wooden animals for the children of dockworkers.
  • Drink. Yes. Openly. No pride in it.
  • Sleep. Never quite restful. Fog. Distant oars. A figure at the edge.

A Boy on the Water

Born and raised in Saltmarsh, son of a dock laborer. Cy learned to row before he learned to read.

Cy & Barker

Partnered with Douglas Barker. Started with a rowboat, worked up to a keelboat. Reliable. Practical. Well-liked among fishermen and working sailors.

Eda

A quiet thing with Eda Oweland through those years. Never public. It faded as her influence grew.

She runs the Council now. They don't speak.
The First Wreck — Twenty Years Ago

A galley wrecked offshore in unusual circumstances. Cy and Barker rowed out repeatedly through the night, saved roughly thirty crew. Local heroes.

Some sailors spoke of strange fog. A vessel sighted before the wreck. The damage looked wrong. The stories were dismissed. He let it go.

The Second Wreck — Ten Years Ago

Another ship. Same waters. The fog came in fast. Barker pushed ahead toward a drifting survivor. When Cy looked back, both were gone.

Barker was never found.

The Dreams

Fog-bound waters. Distant oars. Barker at the edge — never close enough to speak. He calls it guilt.

He may be wrong about what they are.
